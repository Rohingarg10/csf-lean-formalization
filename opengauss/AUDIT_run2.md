## Definitions

- `speed`, `arclength` match Lemma 1.
- `Flow E J`: a moving curve with position, tangent, normal, speed and curvature as separate fields,
  related by: `∂γ/∂u = v T`, `∂γ/∂t = κ n` (equation 2.1), `∂T/∂u = vκ n` (spatial Frenet, which *defines*
  κ), an orthonormal planar frame, differentiability of every field, and symmetry of mixed partials for γ
  and T. Faithful to how the notes use these objects, but these are **assumptions about the data**, not
  consequences of smoothness. No theorem builds a `Flow` from a smooth regular `γ`. Aristotle, by
  contrast, defines T, n, κ from `γ` and derives Frenet and mixed-partial symmetry.
- `ClosedAt` (2π-periodic lift), `EmbeddedAt` (injective on `[0, 2π)`) match the notes.
- `enclosedArea` = Lebesgue measure of the bounded complementary components of the curve's image: the
  true geometric area. `signedArea` is the Green's-formula integral (Aristotle's `enclosedArea`).
- `ClassicalClosedFlow`: CSF on `(0, T₀)`, closed on `[0, T₀)`, jointly continuous fields on `[0, T₀)`,
  regular with a frame at `t = 0`. Same weakening at `t = 0` as Aristotle.

## Theorem 3.0.1 proof outline

`uniform_local_injective` (uniform continuity of T on compact strips gives injectivity on short
parameter intervals) → `shifted_distance_positive` (the squared distance between distinct parameters stays
positive on each compact time interval) via `compact_minimum_principle` (a strict linear-in-time
perturbation argument) and `distanceSq_min_time_nonneg` (at a spatial two-point minimum, CSF cannot
decrease the distance). The strategy is the standard two-point maximum principle, the same as Aristotle's.
A `maxHeartbeats 800000` option is set on one lemma.
